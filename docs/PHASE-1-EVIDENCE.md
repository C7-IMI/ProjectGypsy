## Limits and what is not proven

- **Visual quality is prototype primitives.** The capture proves the slice renders and moves; it is not an art claim.
- **Headless has no renderer** (ADR-001); the logic proof is the headless demo and exported binary, the visual proof is
  the windowed movie-maker capture. Capture needs a desktop session.
- **No UI/HUD, audio, character art, mobile, multiplayer or performance budgets** are in this slice; those remain
  human-directed (ADR-005) or deferred.
- The demo auto-pilots the real `CharacterBody3D`/`move_and_slide()` path, not a teleport; `frames=143` is the
  deterministic 60 Hz physics-frame count.
- The Godot banner precedes the marker on stdout; the game itself prints exactly one marker line.
